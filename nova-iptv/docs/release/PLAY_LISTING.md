# Google Play listing (draft)

**Title:** Nova IPTV Player _(working name – trademark check pending)_

**Short description (≤80):** Fast IPTV player for Android TV: your M3U & Xtream lists, TV guide, archive.

**Full description:**
Nova IPTV is a modern player for Android TV and Google TV, designed for the remote control.

• Add your own M3U/M3U8 playlist (link or file) or an Xtream Codes compatible login
• Live TV with categories, channel numbers, favourites and instant channel switching
• TV guide (EPG) with XMLTV support, day selection and reminders
• Catch-up / archive and live rewind – when your provider supports it (up to 30 days)
• Movies and series with seasons, episodes and resume
• Playback speed, audio track and subtitle selection, aspect ratio
• Fast search across channels, movies, series and programmes
• English, Lithuanian, German and Russian
• Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking; logins encrypted on your device

**Important:** Nova IPTV does not include any channels or content. You need a playlist or subscription
from a provider of your choice. Use only content you are legally entitled to watch.

**Category:** Video Players & Editors · **Content rating:** to be completed in Play Console
**Data safety form:** No data collected; no data shared; data encrypted in transit depends on the
provider's URLs (http/https).
