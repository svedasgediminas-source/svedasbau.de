# Nova IPTV – Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: 2026-10-10. Draft – to be reviewed before publication. "Nova IPTV" is a working name._

Nova IPTV is a media player for Android TV and Google TV. **It does not provide, sell or host any
TV channels, films or other content.** Users add their own playlists from providers of their choice.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting
services. We operate no servers for the app.

## Data stored on your device
- Playlists you add (addresses of M3U files, Xtream-compatible portal addresses, playlist names).
- Login data for Xtream-compatible portals. Usernames and passwords are encrypted with a key held in
  the Android Keystore of your device.
- Downloaded channel lists, TV guide (EPG) data, movie/series catalogues.
- Favourites, watch history and playback position, reminders, app settings.

This data never leaves your device through us. It is excluded from Android cloud backups and
device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## Network connections
The app connects **only** to the addresses you enter (your playlist, portal, EPG and stream
addresses) and to image addresses contained in that data (channel logos, posters). Those servers
are operated by your provider, not by us; their privacy policies apply to what they receive
(for example your IP address and, for Xtream portals, your login).

## Children
The app is not directed at children. A parental setting can hide adult channel groups.
